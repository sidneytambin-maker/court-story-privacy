Record after play, in a safe place

Court Story is designed to help you record and review results, progress and observations after sporting activity, while the details are fresh in your mind. Stop playing and move to a safe place before looking at the screen, entering information or operating any control. Do not use the app while playing a sport, running, moving around an active court, driving, cycling or doing anything that requires your attention for safety. Do not allow spoken feedback, headphones, notifications or vibration to distract you from other people, balls, equipment or your surroundings. If you use scoring or timer tools, operate them only after play has stopped or have a separate, safely positioned non-playing observer operate them.
